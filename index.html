Questions, answered

A few things you may want to know.

What is BioTrail?

BioTrail is a single page for your important links, content, products and contact points. You share one address and visitors choose where to go next.

What makes Guided Trails different?

Guided Trails lets visitors choose a goal such as hiring, shopping or learning. BioTrail can then place the most relevant links first instead of showing everyone the same order.

Can I change the look of my page?

Yes. BioTrail is designed to support your own colours, type, card styles and content so the page feels connected to your personal brand.

Can I start for free?

Yes. You can create a free account and begin building your first page. More options are available on the pricing page.

How can I bring more people to my BioTrail?

Add your BioTrail address to every social bio, your email signature and printed materials. A clear page title and focused links also help visitors understand what to do next.

How many links should I add?

Add the links that support your current goals. Guided Trails can keep a larger collection useful by showing the most relevant choices first.

Do I still need a full website?

Not always. BioTrail can be your simple public home when you mainly need to share links, offers and contact points. A larger website may still help when you need detailed pages or advanced services.
