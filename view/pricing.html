Questions, answered

A few things you may want to know.

Can I really start for free?

Yes. Create a free account and start building your first page today. There is no trial timer and your free page stays yours for as long as you want it.

How does annual billing work?

When you choose annual billing, the plan is paid once per year at a lower per-month rate. You can change or cancel your plan at any time from your account.

What happens when I cancel?

Your page stays live until the end of the paid period. After that, it moves back to Free so you keep your links and can upgrade again whenever you like.

Which plan removes the BioTrail badge?

Pro removes BioTrail branding from your public page. Starter keeps the badge but adds custom colours, more trails and real-time analytics.

Are there any transaction fees on my store?

No. BioTrail does not take a cut of your creator sales. Payment processing fees from your chosen checkout provider still apply as usual.
